About the club

Deadly Drops Pickleball is a premier destination for players in Chula Vista. We run structured tournaments and community events built on sportsmanship, sharpening competitive skills, and an inclusive environment for every age and level.

How it works

  • Register online for tournaments and events.
  • Get matched to a division that fits your skill level (DUPR friendly).
  • Play, compete for medals, and enjoy the community.
