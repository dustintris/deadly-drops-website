How it works

From sign-up to the medal round in three steps.

  1. Register online

    Sign up for a tournament in a few minutes. A personal QR ticket arrives by email for fast check-in on game day.

  2. Get matched

    Find a partner and get placed in a division that fits your level. DUPR ratings keep every bracket fair.

  3. Play for medals

    Round robin play into a medal round, with live scoreboards and a crowd that cheers for every rally.
